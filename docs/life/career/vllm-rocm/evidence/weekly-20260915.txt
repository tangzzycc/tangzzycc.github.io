# CogAgent-18B Optimization Weekly Report / CogAgent-18B 优化周报

**Week ending / 截止日期:** 2026-09-15  
**Hardware / 测试硬件:** AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395, Radeon 8060S (`gfx1151`), single GPU

## English

### Progress This Week

This week optimized CogAgent-18B inference. Compared with last week's AWQ W4A16 result, TTFT decreased from 1164.42 ms to 752.15 ms, an overall reduction of 35.41%.

### Latest AWQ W4A16 Serve Result

**Model:** `CogAgent-18B-AWQ-W4A16`  
**Workload:** one 640×480 image plus 64 English alphabetic characters; greedy decoding with up to 64 output tokens and EOS enabled. Five warmup requests and 20 measured requests were run with `max-num-seqs=1`.

| Test Target | TTFT (P50) | TPOT (P50) | Prefill (P50) | Decode (P50) | End-to-End Latency (P50) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AWQ W4A16 | 752.15 ms | 27.625 ms/token | 369.61 tok/s | 36.20 tok/s | 2491.50 ms |

All 20 requests produced 64 output tokens with identical text.

## 中文

### 本周进展

| 测试对象 | TTFT（P50） | TPOT（P50） | Prefill（P50） | Decode（P50） | 端到端延迟（P50） |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AWQ W4A16 | 752.15 ms | 27.625 ms/token | 369.61 tok/s | 36.20 tok/s | 2491.50 ms |

20 轮请求均输出 64 token，且生成文本完全一致。
